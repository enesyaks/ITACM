# Code of Conduct

ITACM is maintained in the open by people spending their own time on it. This
page says what that requires of everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages and code review.

## What is expected

- **Assume good faith.** A confusing report is usually someone struggling, not
  someone wasting your time. A blunt review comment is usually about the code.
- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This query runs per row" is useful.
  "You clearly don't know SQL" is not, and says nothing about the query.
- **Be patient with people who know less than you do.** Not everyone deploying
  this runs a data centre; a lot of them are one person keeping an office
  running. "Read the docs" is not an answer — point at the part that helps.
- **Let people be wrong without punishing them for it.** Being corrected should
  be cheap here, or nobody will risk contributing.
- **Respect a "no".** Maintainers can decline a feature, and a declined pull
  request is not an insult. Reopening the same argument repeatedly is.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind: insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about someone's
  background, identity, beliefs, appearance, disability, or level of experience
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted personal attention
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to pile on
- Publishing someone's private information — address, workplace, private
  correspondence — without their explicit permission
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or repeatedly posting the same demand
  after it has been answered
- Posting a security vulnerability publicly instead of reporting it privately.
  It endangers everyone running this software — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project happens: this repository, its issues, pull
requests and discussions, and any space where someone is representing ITACM.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **enes@yakisik.com**, with a subject starting
`ITACM conduct:`. Include links or screenshots if you have them.

Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. You will get a reply
within 7 days. You do not need to be the target of the behaviour to report it,
and reporting something in good faith will never count against you — even if,
after looking, the conclusion is that no action is needed.

## What happens next

Depending on what happened and whether it is a pattern:

1. **A private word** — an explanation of what was wrong and why
2. **A public correction** in the thread, with the comment edited or removed
3. **A temporary block** from interacting with the repository
4. **A permanent ban**

Most things stop at step 1. Steps 3 and 4 are for behaviour that is deliberate,
repeated after a warning, or severe on its own — harassment, threats, or
exposing someone's private information.

Enforcement decisions rest with the maintainer. If you think one was wrong, say
so by email; that will be read properly rather than treated as a further
offence.
